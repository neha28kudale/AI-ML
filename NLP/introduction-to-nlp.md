# Introduction to Natural Language Processing (NLP)

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a subfield of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** that focuses on enabling machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language.  
It bridges the gap between human communication and computer understanding.

---

## 1. What NLP Does

NLP powers many everyday applications that process or respond to human language.  
Some common examples include:

- **Text Classification** – Categorizing text into groups (e.g. spam detection in emails).  
- **Sentiment Analysis** – Determining if a statement expresses positive, negative, or neutral feelings (e.g. customer reviews).  
- **Language Translation** – Automatically converting text from one language to another (e.g. Google Translate).  
- **Chatbots and Conversational AI** – Systems like ChatGPT or Alexa that can interact naturally with users.  
- **Named Entity Recognition (NER)** – Identifying key information such as names, dates, and locations in text.  
- **Speech Recognition** – Converting spoken language into text (e.g. voice assistants).  

---

## 2. Core Concepts of NLP

To process text, NLP breaks language down into structured representations that computers can handle.  
The key steps involved include:

- **Tokenization**  
  Splitting text into smaller units, such as words, phrases, or sentences.  
  *Example*:  
  `"I love AI!" → ["I", "love", "AI", "!"]`

- **Stemming & Lemmatization**  
  Reducing words to their root or base form to handle variations.  
  *Example*:  
  `"running", "runs", "ran" → "run"`

- **Vectorization / Embeddings**  
  Converting text into numerical form so that it can be processed by machine learning models.  
  *Example*: using techniques like Bag of Words, TF-IDF, or Word2Vec to translate words into numeric vectors.

- **Language Models**  
  Predicting or generating text by understanding context. They can summarize text, translate languages, or generate coherent sentences.  
  Modern LMs include **BERT**, **GPT**, and **T5**.

## 4. Example 2: Text Classification

- **Input**:  
  `"Exclusive offer! Click here to win a prize."`

- **Task**:  
  Identify if the message is spam or not.

- **Output**:  
  `Spam`

---

## 5. Real-World Applications

- Search engines improve results by understanding query intent.  
- Virtual assistants like Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant process spoken commands.  
- Social media platforms detect hate speech or offensive content.  
- Customer support chatbots automate basic query handling.  
- Translation apps help bridge language barriers across the globe.

---

## Conclusion

NLP combines **linguistics**, **statistics**, and **machine learning** to make computers understand and respond to human language effectively.  
It’s a rapidly growing field at the heart of many AI systems powering today’s digital world.
